%N Maximum number of edges in a simple graph on n nodes that does not contain two edge-disjoint cycles with the same vertex set.
%D 0, 1, 3, 6, 9, 12, 16, 19, 23, 27, 31, 36, 39
%O 1,3
%C Erdős problem 585 asks for the growth rate of a(n); it is open.
%C Equivalently, a(n) is the maximum number of edges of a graph on n nodes in which no vertex set S spans two edge-disjoint Hamiltonian cycles of the induced subgraph. Such a pair needs |S| >= 5, so a(n) = binomial(n,2) for n <= 4.
%C Terms a(1)-a(10) are from the erdosproblemaday working report, which also gives a(11) >= 30. The terms up to a(11) were recomputed by an exhaustive nauty-geng scan and, independently, by a vertex-extension ladder; a(11) = 31 is new. The upper bounds a(10) <= 27 and a(11) <= 31 are certified: every candidate graph with a(n)+1 edges (395166 at n = 10; 2173681 at n = 11, where a(10) = 27 forces minimum degree >= 5) comes with an explicit pair of edge-disjoint cycles on a common vertex set, and a separate verifier checked every pair.
%C Numbers of extremal graphs (isomorphism classes) for n = 1..12: 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 3, 1, 12, 8, 7, 8, 1.
%C a(12) = 36 was found with a certified vertex-extension ladder: every rejected candidate comes with an explicit pair of edge-disjoint cycles on a common vertex set, checked by a separate verifier.
%C a(13) = 39: a graph with 40 edges on 13 nodes has minimum degree 4, 5 or 6 (as a(12) = 36). The extensions of the graphs with 35 or 36 edges on 12 nodes (10093 candidates) and all graphs on 13 nodes with 40 edges and minimum degree 6 (23489426 graphs) contain two edge-disjoint cycles on a common vertex set; again every case has a checked certificate.
%C For 2 <= n <= 13, a(n) = 2*n - 3 + A006856(n-2) (with A006856(0) = 0), and the join of K_2 with an extremal graph of girth >= 5 on n-2 nodes is extremal; for n = 12 the unique extremal graph is the join of K_2 with the Petersen graph.
%C If H is triangle-free and every set of t >= 4 nodes of H spans at most 2*t - 5 edges, then the join of K_2 and H has no two edge-disjoint cycles on a common vertex set (count the edges of the union of the two cycles). Hence a(n) >= 2*n - 3 + A006856(n-2) for n <= 16, a(17) >= 56, a(18) >= 61 and a(n) >= 4*n - 12 for n >= 17. So a(14) >= 43, a(15) >= 48, a(16) >= 52.
%C Pyber, Rödl and Szemerédi showed a(n) >= c*n*log(log(n)); Chakraborti, Janzer, Methuku and Montgomery showed a(n) <= n*(log(n))^O(1).

%H L. Pyber, V. Rödl and E. Szemerédi, Dense graphs without 3-regular subgraphs, J. Combin. Theory Ser. B 63 (1995), 41-54.
%e a(5) = 9: K_5 is the union of two edge-disjoint Hamiltonian cycles, while K_5 minus an edge has only 9 < 10 edges.
