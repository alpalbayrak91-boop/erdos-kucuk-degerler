%N Number of 2-colorings of the 3-subsets of an n-set such that no 4-subset has all four of its 3-subsets of the same color.
%D 1, 1, 2, 14, 512, 118784, 153010788, 638804584320
%O 1,3
%C Labeled R(4,4;3)-good colorings (the two colors are distinguished).
%C a(n) = 0 for n >= 13 since the hypergraph Ramsey number R(4,4;3) = 13 (McKay and Radziszowski, 1991); a(9)..a(12) are positive.
%C a(5) = 512: complementation maps the 3-subsets of a 5-set to its 2-subsets and the four 3-subsets of a 4-set to the four edges at the omitted point, so a(5) is the number of labeled graphs on 5 nodes with no isolated node and no node of degree 4, which is 2^10 - 2*(2^10 - A006129(5)) = 1024 - 2*256.
%C The numbers of isomorphism classes (complements counted separately) for n = 3..8 are 2, 3, 12, 226, 31060, 15854385 (McKay, 2016, Table 1).
%H B. D. McKay and S. P. Radziszowski, The first classical Ramsey number for hypergraphs is computed, Proc. 2nd ACM-SIAM SODA (1991).
%H B. D. McKay, A class of Ramsey-extremal hypergraphs, arXiv:1608.07762 [math.CO], 2016.
%e a(4) = 2^4 - 2 = 14: the four triples of a 4-set may be colored arbitrarily except all red or all blue.
%e a(3) = 2: a single triple, no 4-subset.
